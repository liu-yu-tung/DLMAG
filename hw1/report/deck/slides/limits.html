Errors and limitations

LimitationWhat it means here
Neighbouring decadesthe main error on A (78% of errors); 1990s and 2000s are the weakest classes
English-sung marketsthe main error on B (US, UK, and many German and Italian releases); the model leans on language, which is not the label
Small validation set132 / 102 clips, so differences under about 0.05 S are noise; we selected on train cross-validation instead
No artist IDstrain folds may share artists; grouped folds are a proxy and lower S by 0.017 (A) and 0.037 (B)
Inputmono, 24 kHz (no stereo image, nothing above 12 kHz), one 30 s excerpt per song
Costsubmitted A needs Qwen2-Audio (16 GB download, about 13 GB GPU memory); the fallback (about 6 GB) scores 0.996 vs 1.000
Ported modelsMERT-v2 and MuQ needed load-time patches on transformers 5; checked against repeated loads, not the authors' setup
